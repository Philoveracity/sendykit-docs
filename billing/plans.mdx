## Three commercial lanes

SendyKit has three distinct lanes:

| Lane | Buyer | Pricing shape |
|------|-------|---------------|
| Self-hosted | Operators | one-time perpetual license |
| SaaS | Teams who want managed ops | subscription |
| Premium agent actions | Agents using premium surfaces | machine-paid per action |

## Self-hosted

Self-hosted uses:
- Standard
- Pro
- future-major upgrade ladders
- Sovereign Infinite as the premium top tier

See [Self-Hosted Editions](/self-hosted/editions).

## SaaS

Current canonical SaaS price points in product docs are:
- Starter — $39/mo
- Growth — $99/mo
- Scale — $249/mo
- Enterprise — custom

## Add-ons

Examples of metered add-ons:
- SMTP Pool — 10K emails
- SMTP Pool — 50K emails
- AI Credits — 500

## What not to assume

Do not assume:
- self-hosted is free
- SaaS is the only real product
- direct x402 is the current public billing path
