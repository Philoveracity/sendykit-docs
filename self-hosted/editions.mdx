## Overview

Self-hosted SendyKit uses two core editions:
- **Standard**
- **Pro**

Both are perpetual licenses.

The difference is not subscription vs non-subscription.
The difference is feature depth and site entitlement.

## Upgrade lanes

Self-hosted pricing is organized by included future major upgrades:

### Standard
- Current + 1 — $349

### Pro
- Current + 1 — $699
- Current + 3 — $1,299
- Current + 5 — $1,999
- Sovereign Infinite — $5,999

## How to choose

Choose **Standard** when:
- you want the exoskeleton layer on one install
- you do not need advanced AI/automation features yet
- you want the lowest entry point

Choose **Pro** when:
- you already know you want deeper operator automation
- you run multiple installs
- AI authoring and advanced orchestration matter now

Choose **Sovereign Infinite** when:
- you want the permanent top-tier hedge
- you expect SendyKit to stay in your operating model long-term

## Rule of thumb

For most serious self-hosted buyers, **Current + 3** is the practical center of gravity.
